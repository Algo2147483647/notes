# Sale


- **Standard Product Unit (SPU)**: An abstract template of product information that encapsulates the core, common, and immutable attributes of a product, representing a standardized collection of information.
- **Stock Keeping Unit (SKU)**: The smallest unit available for inventory and sales, representing a concrete instance of an SPU. It includes additional sales-specific dimensions (such as color, size, flavor, capacity, etc.) as well as inventory and pricing information.
- Category


A Sale Item comprises one or more SKUs and defines the rules for selling them. While SPUs and SKUs describe "what" the product is, the Sale Item describes "how" it is sold. Kit/Bundle: Combines multiple independent SKUs into a single package sold at a bundled price.
